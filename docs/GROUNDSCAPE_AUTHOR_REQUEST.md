Subject: Research access to a small complete Sat2GroundScape subset

Dear Ningli Xu and Professor Qin,

I am developing a small research project on generating structurally faithful ground-level building views from overhead imagery. Your CVPR 2025 paper, Satellite to GroundScape, is a central reference for this work.

I inspected the public Perspective and Panorama releases. They include rendered satellite conditions and camera metadata, while the real ground photographs are referenced by panorama IDs. Could you advise whether a small complete subset, initially around 100-500 locations, is available through an arrangement that permits research model training and evaluation? I would also appreciate clarification of the applicable terms for obtaining and using the ground targets.

For an experiment with one overhead RGB image as the scene input, are the corresponding original satellite tiles, their mapping to ground cameras, and the camera scale/orientation conventions available? If available, aligned meshes/depth, official split files, training code and pretrained weights would also help us compare fairly with your work.

The initial study will run on a laptop and examine building-boundary errors, with larger experiments only after feasibility is established. I can provide any research details required for access and will follow the applicable attribution and data-sharing conditions.
